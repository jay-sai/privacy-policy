Privacy Policy for Opi suomea — FinnishFlashcards

Last updated: November 2026

1. Overview

Opi suomea — FinnishFlashcards ("the App") is an educational language-learning application designed for children and families. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

2. Personal Data & Information Collection

The App does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or sensitive user data.

  • No user accounts, logins, or profiles.
  • No tracking, profiling, or behavioral analytics.
  • All learning progress and preferences remain strictly on your local device.

3. Third-Party Analytics & Advertising

The App does not contain any third-party advertising, advertising identifiers, or analytics tracking SDKs.

4. In-App Purchases

The App offers an optional one-time in-app purchase to unlock all card packs. All purchase transactions are handled directly and securely through Google Play In-App Billing. We do not collect, process, or store credit card numbers or financial details.

5. Children's Privacy (Families Policy & COPPA Compliance)

The App is designed for children and families. Because we do not collect any personal data from any user, we do not collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our developer support team via the contact details provided on our Google Play Store listing.
